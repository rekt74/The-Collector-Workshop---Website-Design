A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Before we begin.

Where should I start with my restoration?

Start with a conversation. Share your car’s make, model, condition, and the outcome you have in mind so the workshop can discuss a suitable assessment.

Can I discuss a smaller restoration project?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe the work you’re considering, whether it’s one area of the car or a more extensive restoration. Scope and suitability can then be confirmed.

How long does a restoration take?

Timing depends on the car’s condition, the agreed scope, and parts availability. A realistic timeline should be discussed after an assessment.
